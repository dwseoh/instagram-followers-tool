How to export your Instagram data
  1. In the Instagram app, go to Settings → Accounts Center.
  2. Tap Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  3. Tap Download or transfer information, pick your account, then choose Some of your information.
  4. Select Followers and following only — that keeps the file small and the wait short.
  5. Choose Download to device. Set format to JSON (not HTML) and date range to All time.
  6. Submit the request. Instagram emails you a download link — usually within minutes, sometimes up to 48 hours.
  7. Download the .zip and drop it above. No need to unzip it — but if you'd rather, the files you need are followers_1.json and following.json inside connections/followers_and_following/.

JSON format is required. An HTML export won't work here.

Privacy Policy

Your data never leaves this device. There is no server, no account, no cloud compute, and no analytics.

  • Your export is read and compared by JavaScript running in your own browser.
  • Your usernames and swipe progress are kept in this browser's local storage, on this device only, so you can close the tab and pick up where you left off.
  • Nothing is uploaded, transmitted, logged, or shared — there is nowhere for it to go.

Hit Clear data any time to erase everything from local storage. Using a shared computer? Clear it when you're done.

This tool never touches Instagram's API. This tool does not break Instagram's Terms of Service.
